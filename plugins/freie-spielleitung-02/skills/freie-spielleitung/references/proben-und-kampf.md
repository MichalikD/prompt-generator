# Checks and combat

## Decide whether to roll

Roll uncertain actions with meaningful consequences. Clear actions succeed; impossible actions do not become possible through a lucky die. Use relevant expertise for basic knowledge. Chosen check frequency changes opportunities for risks, not SG or mandatory roll counts. Tone does not change numerical difficulty.

Before a check, state the intended objective, attribute, applicable talent/equipment, SG and foreseeable stakes in German meta text. Do not reveal hidden facts just to announce risk. Preparation must have a stated effect: it permits the action, lowers SG, or gives advantage. Genuine help can do the same; no invented support +2. Count a cause once.

Resolve the whole declared action. Use multiple stages only if they involve distinct decisions/stakes, not every grip and step. A successful preparation does not merely unlock another identical check; carry its effect into the actual objective. If the player declares several routine actions, compress them until the next meaningful uncertainty or decision.

## Formula and dice

W20 + attribute + one applicable talent (+2) + one equipment bonus (at most +2) ≥ SG: success.

| SG | Difficulty |
| --- | --- |
| 8 | easy |
| 11 | normal |
| 14 | demanding |
| 17 | hard |
| 20 | exceptional |

Advantage: 2W20, take the higher. Disadvantage: 2W20, take the lower. Multiple sources do not stack; advantage and disadvantage cancel. No double reward/penalty for one cause. Natural 1/20 receive plausible effects, not automatic death, impossible feats or unannounced critical damage.

With player rolls, request raw dice for their character, wait, then calculate; do not invent the player’s raw roll. The GM handles NPCs/enemies using an actual random tool if available. Without one, label chat rolls as simulated once. Never pretend a tool was used, alter a result or reroll for a preferred outcome.

Open display includes enemies and raw arithmetic separately from fiction, e.g. “W20 12+Geschick 3+Talent 2=17 ≥ SG14: Erfolg.” Show both advantage/disadvantage dice. With compact display, show outcome and changes; provide details on request. The previously announced stakes and SG remain binding.

## Failure and retries

Failure changes the situation according to the announced stakes: harm, lost time, exposure, spent resources, changed position, relationship damage, a missed opportunity, or success at a price. Apply the consequence promptly and continue from the changed state. Failure can end an attempt or an adventure. Do not replace it with a new prerequisite and another near-identical roll until safe success is inevitable.

Same-method retries are allowed when fiction permits. A missed rope throw can be thrown again while the victim continues sinking; the throw need not be cosmetically redesigned. Each attempt costs the time/action/resources the situation requires, and consequences accumulate. If nothing can change and no consequence is possible, do not farm rolls: resolve the routine or state why no further attempt is available. A definitive failure may close an opportunity; a retry is not an entitlement.

Do not secretly protect victims or the hero until the desired roll appears. Warn of foreseeable lethal stakes, then allow them to happen. Do not introduce arbitrary hazards after a roll, invalidate an established safe anchor to manufacture tension, or remove known safety measures to justify a consequence. If you announced inconsistent stakes, correct them before rolling; if you made an error, restore unfair costs.

## Combat

Offer plausible fighting, negotiation, flight, deception and positioning. NPCs pursue their goals; they need not fight to the death. Explain positions, cover, reach and visible intentions before consequential choices. One action plus movement per round. Use the fiction for clear order; otherwise each participant rolls W20+Geschick without talent/equipment bonuses, highest first. Reroll only tied participants and keep the order until the situation meaningfully changes. Each participant acts once per round.

Attacks use the normal check formula against target defense. Default defense = 10+Geschick, plus 1 for appropriate armor. No additional routine defensive roll after an enemy’s attack. Resolve both sides’ attacks openly: announce target, bonus, target defense and advantage/disadvantage, obtain the raw roll, determine hit, apply damage and show remaining LP and changed position. A special action to protect someone resolves its announced danger once; do not independently reroll that same danger to undo the result. Fix and show weapon/enemy LP, attack, defense and damage before using them; justify stronger values rather than changing them to fit the roll. Two weapons grant no extra action unless an agreed ability says so. A missed attack grants no free counterattack. Assistance follows the normal preparation rules and costs a plausible action; no unlimited free attacks from helpers.

Base damage: light 2, ordinary 3, heavy 4, for both sides. At 0 LP a creature is incapacitated; circumstances determine rescue or death. Announce foreseeable lethal danger, not guaranteed survival. Do not add unannounced critical multipliers.

A safe short rest restores +2 LP once per rest phase; full safe rest restores full LP. Treat injuries when appropriate; explain any separate effects, recovery and treatment before they govern play. No hidden recovery penalties.

## Optional dice

Use only selected extra dice; otherwise fixed base damage. Explain purpose/table before rolling. Follow the chosen roller/display rules, without attribute/talent modifiers for these extra rolls. Attribute checks remain W20.

- Selected W4 for light, W6 for ordinary, W8 for heavy damage: if roll ≤ floor(W/4), base−1; if roll > W−floor(W/4), base+1; otherwise base. Apply symmetrically to both sides. Selecting one damage die does not activate the other categories.
- Selected W10: ten similarly valuable finds, with 0=10 when rolled alone.
- Selected W12: twelve world events, not mandatory detours to extend an adventure.
- Selected D% requires D10: tens 00–90 plus units 0–9; 00+0=100. Announce the chance first; result ≤ chance triggers the event.
