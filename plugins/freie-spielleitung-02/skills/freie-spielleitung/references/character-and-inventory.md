# Character and inventory

## Starting character

Preserve a confirmed sheet. If creating one, use the chosen method: player-entered values with validation, or a short idea expanded into a draft for confirmation. Do not infer a whole stereotyped life from an occupation. Leave unspecified origin, goals and relationships open; list only explicitly requested exclusions. Open details may emerge later without contradicting confirmed facts or assigning the player choices and feelings.

Attributes are integers from −1 to +3 at creation, totaling +5:

- Strength: strength, endurance and forceful weapon use.
- Dexterity: precision, movement and stealth.
- Intellect: knowledge, analysis, planning and technology.
- Insight: perception, insight and intuition.

Start with 10/10 HP, two distinct talents each granting +2, a situational weakness, and fitting equipment within eight carrying slots. Attribute advancement caps at +5. Preserve the original starting sheet separately from current values when possible.

## Useful talents, powers and weaknesses

A talent describes a recognizable useful domain: its name and scope should agree across plausible applications. Avoid a talent that works only for one task, surface, material or tool. A movement talent can apply on different kinds of difficult footing; a rescue talent should not cover only fastening a knot while excluding the relevant rescue work. These are scope examples, not required talents. Do not grant a bonus to every action or the whole attribute. Choose the attribute from the declared approach; talents need not be tied to one attribute unless their nature requires it. Apply at most one +2 talent bonus per check.

Talent scope can include fitting abilities learned later. A spellcasting talent applies to checks for later learned spells within its domain as well as starting spells; it does not grant additional spells, free effects or exemptions from costs. New abilities and changes of nature can be agreed through development. A starting sheet describes current capabilities, not a permanent ceiling or a promise of specific future powers. Begin with worthwhile capabilities; apply Development and Saves for timely new powers, techniques and equipment rather than relying only on slow attribute increases.

Species, occupation and requested power scale should be mechanically and fictionally recognizable. Create capabilities worth using in exploration, conflict and other fitting situations, not a collection of harmless conveniences. An offensive mage can begin with damaging magic; an established powerful necromancer needs powers that express that idea. Mortality is vulnerability to death, not a requirement for weak magic. If the requested concept conflicts with the agreed creation rules, clarify the conflict or propose an explicit deviation before confirmation instead of quietly reducing the concept to a novice. Preserve confirmed sheets; changes require agreement.

For each exceptional ability, define what it accomplishes, range/reach, targets or area, duration where relevant, resource cost, resolution and consequential limitations. Boundaries make its use predictable; they do not make it harmless. Dangerous effects can be powerful and have environmental or social consequences. A fire attack can burn enemies and ignite suitable material; neither universal fireproof scenery nor an automatic entire building fire follows from every use. Use Checks and Combat for damaging powers. A spellcasting talent improves applicable checks but is not a spell list.

Magic users normally start with three resource points, independently of the number or potency of their abilities; agree spending, failure cost and regeneration for the concept. For a new combat caster, propose at least one reliable resource-free magical attack or comparably useful repeatable combat option. A simple magical attack defaults to one visible target within 15 m, one action, the normal attack check and light damage 2, with its element and consequential limits defined on the sheet. It can miss and is not an extra attack. Focus pays for stronger, area or other exceptional effects rather than the mere ability to keep playing the concept. These are creation proposals, not automatic new abilities for confirmed characters. Honour a player's deliberate different design. Default recovery for a new three-point pool is +1 after a safe uninterrupted one-hour short rest, once between full rests, and full after a safe eight-hour rest; record it explicitly or agree another schedule. The default budget is a proposal, not a covert ceiling on a requested epic concept. Use an explicitly agreed deviation when required. Cyberware bonuses count as equipment bonuses. A world or idea that excludes supernatural abilities remains binding; leave unneeded resource pools empty. No single ability automatically solves every kind of problem.

A weakness needs an observable trigger and clear effect, with mitigation if relevant. It should be situational, not near-constant impairment or a feeling invented by the GM. Ordinary supply scarcity is not an extra personal weakness. Add further weaknesses only when the player’s idea explicitly requires them. Special food needs remain real but are abstract until consequences matter; explain a hunger/resource mechanic before introducing it.

Keep HP, injuries and visible symptoms distinct. Use the common recovery and incapacitation rules in Checks and Combat for player characters, companions and enemies alike. Lasting injuries are an optional agreed addition, not a default exemption for NPCs or a hidden penalty for the player. Explain trigger, effect, treatment and recovery before introducing one. Do not silently add penalties or invent complications to neutralize good preparation.

## Inventory and equipment

Eight carried slots. Ordinary equipment uses one; bulky objects may use more; small things can be bundled. Clothing/trifles may use zero; weapons count. Record quantity and slots per unit. Respect weight, size and physique; a carried container or vehicle is not infinite storage. External storage is separate. Quest objects may occupy slots; when full, exchange, store or leave something behind.

Prefer useful versatile gear and compact supplies to piles of scrap. Equipment may permit an action without granting a numerical bonus. A justified situational equipment bonus is at most +2, only one per check. Preparation/help uses enabling, lower DC or advantage, not an invented extra +2. Do not count the same cause twice.

When awarding money, unusual equipment, contacts or faction standing, explain practical buying power, uses and limits when relevant. Do not force a price catalogue into every scene. Purchases and preparation can offer real progress.

## Sheet and JSON template

Use the generator’s format when supplied: identity; attributes; talents; abilities with range/targets/damage/effect/cost/limits; weaknesses with trigger/effect/mitigation; vitals with HP, resources and injuries; carried inventory; finances; optional special needs; confirmed/open/excluded background; development. For new structured abilities, range, targets (count or area) and damage are descriptive strings, including conditional values; damage can explicitly be None. An explicitly empty range or string 0 means no distance limit, not exemption from sight, line of effect or other conditions. Touch must be written explicitly, not as 0. Older supplied abilities lacking these separate fields retain the range, targets and damage defined in effect/limits; missing fields never mean unlimited reach. Do not silently reinterpret, duplicate conflicting values or upgrade a confirmed power when displaying these fields. If supplied values conflict in a consequential use, clarify the intended value before resolution. The field split changes presentation, not attack checks, damage defaults, actions, resources or equipment rules. Money belongs in finances rather than an ability resource pool. Do not treat empty fields as prohibitions. Confirmed values define the starting state, not a ban on later development.

For a current sheet, a learned power goes in abilities with its complete usable effect; a changed power replaces its current definition, not the original starting sheet. Put an item's passive effect or modest damage upgrade in its weapon.feature/description and actual damage band, with any activation requirements. If an item grants an activated power, also describe it in abilities, explicitly naming the required item in its effect/limits and any shared uses; this is the same effect, not a second independent benefit. Losing the item removes access to that item power, not permanently learned skills. Keep damage/range definitions consistent across both records. weapon.damage remains light/ordinary/heavy; a separately agreed exceptional modifier or power belongs in feature or the ability's descriptive damage/effect, not a new enum code. A stronger item need not have new drawbacks simply to offset every improvement.

Development records improvements already received, not a mandatory advancement plan. An empty starting history means no advancement yet, not that advancement is unavailable. Apply the milestone rules during play, retain the original sheet and record agreed improvements in the current sheet.

For a requested JSON export, preserve the supplied schema/field names, distinguish start and current sheets, retain the original when available, and mark the draft unconfirmed until the player reviews it. A readable sheet remains valid for ordinary play; do not require JSON just to start.

## Weapon records

For new weapons use the optional inventory `weapon` object: `damage` is light/ordinary/heavy, while `range`, `handling`, `feature` and `limits` are nonempty strings defining normal/maximum reach, hands/space, a useful distinction and its restriction. Keep general description, quantity, carrying slots and check bonus. A heavy weapon may need more carried slots; do not silently change them on an existing confirmed item. See Checks and Combat for damage dice and slim profile resolution. Do not duplicate contradictory numerical properties in prose, give every firearm identical use, or reinterpret old weapons solely from their names. A profile is an agreed proposal at creation, not an automatic replacement for confirmed equipment. A combat specialist can later learn a specified maneuver or improve effective power through agreed development; the one-action default and fixed talent +2 do not themselves grant extra attacks.
